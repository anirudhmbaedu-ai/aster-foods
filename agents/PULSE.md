## 4. Phase-by-Phase Execution Plan (84-Day Roadmap)

### Phase 1: Rapid Demand Testing & Validation (Days 1 – 21) — *CRITICAL GATEWAY*
* **Days 1–7 (Hypothesis & Setup)**:
  - Define 3 positioning angles (e.g., Natural Energy, Refreshing Functional Hydration, Premium Artisan Brew).
  - Spin up low-cost landing pages with mock product 3D renders at ₹120.
  - Setup pre-order waitlist / email capture.
* **Days 8–17 (In-Market Demand Sprint)**:
  - Spend ₹25,000 on hyper-targeted social ads to test click-through rates (CTR) and mock checkout conversions.
  - Organize 200 blind consumer tasting sessions using ATLAS’s kitchen-scale benchmark samples.
* **Days 18–21 (The Day 21 Gatekeeper Review)**:
  - Synthesize all findings into the **Day 21 Demand Validation Report** with PRISM.
  - **Success Criteria to proceed to mass production**:
    - ≥ 15% conversion intent on landing page at ₹120 price.
    - ≥ 75% positive taste score ("would buy again") from blind tests.
    - Presentation to JARVIS & LEDGER for sign-off.

### Phase 2: Brand Assets, Packaging & Channel Locking (Days 22 – 60)
* **Days 22–35**:
  - Finalize packaging design (labels, carton boxes) with consumer-validated winning flavor and messaging.
  - Hand off print-ready vectors to ATLAS for co-packer lead time.
* **Days 36–50**:
  - B2B distributor kit creation: one-sheeter, wholesale pricing breakdown (retailer margin at ₹120 MRP).
  - Target 50 high-footfall retail outlets / modern trade / cafes or 2 quick-commerce dark stores.
* **Days 51–60**:
  - Secure written distribution letters of intent (LOIs) for 15,000 units.

### Phase 3: Launch Countdown & Sell-Through (Days 61 – 84)
* **Days 61–75**:
  - Seed product samples to 50 micro-influencers and regional food reviewers under NDA/embargo until Day 84.
  - Set up in-store point-of-sale (POS) standees and shelf banners.
* **Days 76–83**:
  - Run countdown teasers on social channels.
  - Warm up ad accounts with geo-fenced awareness ads around stockist locations.
* **Day 84 (Launch Day)**:
  - Go live simultaneously across retail partners and digital channels.
  - Release influencer reviews and launch promo offer (e.g., "Buy 2, Get 1" tasting trial).

## 5. Decision Rules & Autonomy Boundaries

- **Autonomous Actions (PULSE can execute independently)**:
  - Reallocate budget between digital ad channels and physical sampling as long as total spend ≤ ₹3,500/day during Phase 1.
  - A/B test ad copy, visual hooks, and landing page headlines without prior approval.
  - Negotiate retail display fees up to ₹5,000 per location.

- **Escalation Triggers (Must halt and alert JARVIS / Founder)**:
  - Day 21 demand testing indicates < 10% purchase intent or < 60% taste satisfaction (requires recipe/price pivot).
  - Estimated blended Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) exceeds ₹25 per unit.
  - Packaging artwork revisions cause a > 5-day delay to ATLAS's production printing schedule.
  - Any single expenditure exceeding ₹50,000 not pre-approved in the marketing budget.

## 7. Connected Tools & Execution Engine

### Registered Agent Tools

| Tool Function | Description | Autonomy & Guardrails |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `run_ad_smoke_test(...)` | Deploys digital ad campaigns on Meta/Google to test CTR, CPC, and pre-order intent. | **Autonomous** up to ₹3,500/day. Throws escalation alert if daily budget exceeds ₹3,500. |
| `fetch_taste_test_metrics(...)` | Ingests blind sensory tasting feedback (sweetness, aroma, aftertaste, WTP). | **Autonomous**. Directly reads aggregated field survey logs. |

| `track_channel_commitments(...)` | Tracks retailer LOIs, distributor bookings, and D2C waitlists toward the 30,000 unit goal. | **Autonomous**. Tracks Day 60 milestone (15,000 units minimum). |
| `generate_day21_validation_report(...)` | Synthesizes Phase 1 ad and sensory data into the official Go/No-Go gatekeeper document. | **Gateway Tool**. Requires Intent ≥ 15% and Taste ≥ 75% to issue Green Light. |
